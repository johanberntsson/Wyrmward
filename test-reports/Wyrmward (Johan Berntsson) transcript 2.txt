> help




This game is much like the adventure games Infocom wrote in the eighties. The
player types what he or she wants to do, and the game tells what happens.

You cannot finish the game without using magic. You can read spells from scrolls
or your spellbook by typing "cast SPELL on SOMETHING/SOMEONE" To see what spells
you have you can type "read spellbook"

[Please press SPACE.]



In this game you control the main character by typing commands that tell him
what to do. Examples of such commands are:

> look (look around you)
> north (go north from where you are)
> inventory (show a list of what you are carrying)
> take the map
> examine it ("it" here refers to the map)
> give the map to the woman
> wizard, open the door (ask someone to do something)
> ask the wizard about the map
> throw the knife at the black bird

There are some common abbreviations:

g = again (repeat the last command)
l = look
i = inventory
x = examine
z = wait (lets the game clock tick one step)

The possible directions are all compass directions plus up and down, and can be
abbreviated: "n" means north, "u" means up, "sw" means southwest, and so on.

A game can be saved with "save" and a saved game restored with "restore". You
can start over with "restart" and quit with "quit". "score" shows how many
points you have. If you make a mistake you can undo one step with "undo".

[Please press SPACE.]
